Website information

Privacy notice

Last updated November 5, 2026

Scope

This notice describes the Jam Roc public restaurant website. It does not describe every interaction with the restaurant in person or by telephone, the separate staff portal, or another company’s website. Online ordering links take you to DoorDash or Grubhub. This site does not offer customer accounts, payment collection, a newsletter sign-up, or a contact submission form.

Information used by the website

  • Browsing and connection information. Loading pages and live information sends technical requests to our hosting and application providers. These requests can include your IP address, browser information, requested resources, and connection times. Providers may process technical logs to deliver and protect their services.
  • Menu previews. The public site does not save menu or custom drink selections in browser storage or send those selections to the restaurant or an ordering provider.
  • Pickup information. The pickup feature is designed to display a customer’s first name, order number, and preparing/ready status publicly while an order is active. Anyone visiting the board can see those details; it is not a private order lookup. Order notes are available to authorized staff, not displayed on the public board.
  • Direct inquiries. If you call or write to the restaurant, the information you choose to provide is used to address your inquiry. That communication happens outside the website.

Providers and external destinations

GitHub Pages hosts the public pages, and Convex supplies live menu, event, settings, and pickup features. These providers handle the technical data needed to operate their services. Read GitHub’s privacy statement and Convex’s privacy policy.

Directions links open Google Maps. The provider credit links to REIMAGE BUSINESS SOLUTIONS. Those destinations receive information when you visit them and have their own practices. Choosing DoorDash or Grubhub takes you to that provider’s site, where its privacy and payment practices apply. This website does not transmit menu selections to either service or collect payment-card details. Those external orders are not automatically sent to this website’s pickup board.

Advertising and tracking

The current public website has no advertising pixels, audience analytics scripts, or affiliate tracking links. It does not send saved menu choices to advertising networks or provide a targeted-advertising or personal-data-sale feature. See Cookies & browser storage for what is saved on your device.

Storage and security

Saved choices persist in your browser until you remove them or clear the relevant site storage; they have no automatic expiry. Removing an order from the public pickup board does not mean its underlying record has been deleted. The application does not currently configure an automatic deletion schedule for order history. Hosting and service logs follow the providers’ own practices.

The application restricts staff access and encrypts stored customer names and preparation notes. No website or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You can clear browser site data to remove any menu choices saved by an earlier version of the site. Contact the restaurant to ask about information it holds about you, request access, correction, deletion or a copy, or raise a privacy concern. Rights and exceptions depend on applicable law. We may need information to verify your request; provide only what is necessary. If a request is denied, ask the restaurant to review that decision through the same contact route.

Contact Jam Roc Restaurant & Lounge at (860) 906-1812 or write to 3395 Main St, Hartford, CT 06120. Please identify the website page and the subject of your request. Do not send payment-card details or sensitive personal information.

Connecticut residents can learn about applicable rights and complaints through the Connecticut Attorney General’s privacy resources. This notice does not claim that every statutory right applies to every visitor or business.

The site does not offer services requiring children to submit personal information. If you believe a child’s information has been provided, contact the restaurant. This notice will be updated when website practices change.
