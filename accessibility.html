Accessibility statement

Last updated November 5, 2026

Our approach

We aim to make Jam Roc’s menu, location, events, and pickup information usable by people with disabilities. We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA as a design reference and improvement target. A comprehensive independent accessibility audit has not been completed, and we do not claim verified conformance.

Features available

The site includes keyboard-operable links and buttons, visible keyboard focus, a skip-to-content link on the main website, labeled menu controls, text alternatives for content images, and responsive layouts. Its animation styles account for reduced-motion preferences. Policy pages and the restaurant’s basic contact information can be read without JavaScript.

Current limitations

The interactive menu, custom drink builder, dialogs, and live pickup updates require JavaScript. Assistive-technology behavior across all browsers and devices has not been fully tested. Text shown within food posters or storefront photographs is not a substitute for the written menu. Live information may also be unavailable during a connection interruption.

Third-party destinations such as maps and DoorDash and Grubhub checkout have their own accessibility features and limitations. This statement covers the public website, not an assessment of the restaurant’s physical premises or the separate staff portal.

Get help or report a barrier

If you cannot use part of the website, call (860) 906-1812 for help with menu information, visiting, events, or pickup. You can also write to Jam Roc Restaurant & Lounge, 3395 Main St, Hartford, CT 06120.

When reporting a website problem, include the page, what you were trying to do, and, if you wish, the browser or assistive technology involved. Tell us your preferred way to receive a response. You do not need to disclose a disability or medical information to request assistance.

This statement will be revised as the website and its accessibility review change. Its structure follows W3C guidance on accessibility statements.
