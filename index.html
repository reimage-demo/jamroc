Our story · Est. 2017

A taste of Jamaica.
A place in Hartford.

Founded in 2017 by owner Courtney G. Martin, Jam Roc Restaurant & Lounge brings the flavors and spirit of Jamaica to Main Street in Hartford. From jerk chicken and curry goat to oxtail and brown stew chicken, our menu celebrates the comforting island dishes that bring people together. With warm hospitality, music, and a welcoming atmosphere, Jam Roc is a place to share a meal, catch up with friends, and enjoy a taste of home.
