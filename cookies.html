Website information

Cookies & browser storage

Last updated November 5, 2026

What the current site uses

The public website’s code does not set cookies or load advertising or audience analytics trackers. It does not save menu or custom drink selections in browser local storage. Orders are placed through DoorDash or Grubhub.

Previously saved menu choices

An earlier version of this site used a browser storage entry named jamroc-picks for menu selections. The current site no longer reads or updates that entry. Any existing entry remains in your browser until you or your browser clears it.

How to manage it

Use your browser’s site-data settings to clear any previously saved choices. Browser storage settings may group this GitHub Pages site with other pages on the same hosting domain. Clearing that group can affect other sites there. Close or reload tabs running an older version of Jam Roc so they do not save an older list again.

You can restrict site storage through your browser settings. The menu remains browsable. Private browsing usually clears saved data when the private session ends.

Other services

Hosting and live-data requests still occur even if you block storage. They can expose technical connection information to the service providers, as described in our Privacy notice. External destinations may use their own cookies when you visit them. This page does not describe the separate staff portal.

There are currently no optional advertising or analytics categories to turn on or off, so this site does not display a consent banner for those categories. We will update this notice and any required controls if the website’s storage practices change.
